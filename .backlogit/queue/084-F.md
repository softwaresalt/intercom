Feature release unit for Shipment 077-S (STASH 026CF750 [PR #179, 059-S / 067-F], STASH 7EF4DA98 & FE0D5ECD [PR #181, 060-S / 068-F], and STASH 86BE6345, 21F9D21A [review thread PRRT_kwDOTPuhps6wl2fF], & 6617141A [PR #195, 065-S / 072-F]). Adds ConnectOptions.Validate atomic hub-step credential revalidation at Hub.admission/completeConnect and Hub.processAction; enforces Feedback clearing/bounding and local_only_action refusals via decodedAction in internal/transport; offloads over-cap 503 writes from limitListener.Accept to a non-blocking 64-slot FIFO refusal queue and counts over-cap 503s (connection_cap_exceeded), wrong-path 404s (wrong_path), and library websocket.Accept refusals (invalid_handshake, validating RFC 6455 Sec-WebSocket-Version/Key before Hub.Connect) while excluding benign draining 503s; adds transport-layering-boundary and wsclient-standalone-boundary depguard rules in .golangci.yml; rejects URL fragments and opaque URLs in wsclient.Dial and adds config<->transport, wire-constant drift, and layering conformance tests; rate-limits pumpRejects to at most one prog.Send per 250ms interval in internal/app; and updates ADR MVP-2 and the operator runbook.
