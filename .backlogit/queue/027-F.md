BLOCKED - B2 (Stage artifact handoff). Stage backlog/shipment artifacts committed on chore/stage-* are absent from main when Ship branches from origin/main, so a shipment referenced by a Stage artifact is unreachable at Ship claim time. Deliberation finding F-5: B2 is SEPARABLE and NOT universally circular - _orchestrator.agent.md:270-297 already defines a working Staging Artifact Merge Gate (commit to chore/stage-{shipment_id}, push, open PR, wait for merge, verify via git show origin/main:.backlogit/queue/{shipment_id}.md), demonstrated in working use by PRs #59, #60 and #61. B2's real defects are narrower than the Option 1 decision recorded: (a) item 3 names NO ACTOR, so no role is bound to execute the gate; (b) item 3(e) directs 'Attempt a direct push to main first', which conflicts with P-009 (no direct pushes to a protected default branch) and with P-010 role boundaries for both Stage and Ship. Scope: assign an explicit actor to Orchestrator Step 1.5 item 3 and remove or fence the direct-push arm. NOT gated on 026-F (O-5) - may proceed independently. Sequencing preference only, not a hard blocking edge.
