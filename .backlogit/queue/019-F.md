STRAND A - STAGE ARTIFACT PERSISTENCE ROUTE EXECUTION. DEFERRED, BLOCKED, READINESS-LOCKED.

WHY THIS FEATURE EXISTS. 018-F delivers the PROHIBITION half of the Stage default-branch policy gap: Stage may not commit on the default branch, may create chore/stage-{scope-slug}, and must pass a fail-closed Step 1.9 gate before any tracked mutation. It deliberately delivers NO persistence EXECUTION. Until this feature lands, a Stage session terminates with bounded artifacts UNCOMMITTED on the Stage branch, the installed Orchestrator Step 1.5 cannot discover that branch (its step 2 is main-scoped: "git log origin/main..main" is empty when Stage worked on a side branch), and the pipeline FAILS CLOSED at Step 1.5 step 4 with STAGING_GATE_FAIL unless an operator or Orchestrator performs the manual sequence recorded in 018-F goals. This feature replaces that manual sequence with a specified, executable one.

SCOPE (four tasks): 019.007-T handback record emission with concrete artifact paths; 019.008-T no-shipment terminal reachability (failures still HALT); 019.009-T the Stage artifact commit step with out-of-root path safety; 019.004-T Orchestrator Step 1.5 authorization, branch discovery and concrete post-merge verification.

NOT IN THIS FEATURE. The reusable mechanical enforcement platform - structure-aware direct-push detector, fixture corpus, go test -json event parsing, regeneration-resistant CI wiring, CODEOWNERS and divergence ledger - is STRAND B, feature 020-F, shipment 019-S. The two strands were separated on 2026-09-12 because the old 019-F mixed PERSISTENCE SEMANTICS with REUSABLE ENFORCEMENT INFRASTRUCTURE, which have different consumers, different blast radius and different review needs. Neither strand depends on the other: after the split each depends only on 018.008-T and its own internal chain.

MECHANICAL READINESS LOCK - NOT PROSE. Shipment 018-S is status BLOCKED and carries a blocks dependency edge to readiness-gate shipment 020-S. Both conditions independently make it ineligible for Orchestrator Step 2 selection: Step 2 triggers on QUEUED shipments only, and its explicit pre-claim re-check treats an unshipped blocking predecessor as a HARD eligibility gate regardless of queue position. Ship Step 0.5 item 1b independently rejects a shipment that is not queued or active, so a mis-routed claim also fails closed. Verified: "backlogit queue view --type shipment" lists 018-S nowhere.

HOW STAGE RECONSTITUTES THIS FEATURE. Only after 021.001-T records a REVIEWED decision selecting a valid harness execution model, Stage (never Ship, never the Orchestrator) performs, in order: (1) backlogit dep remove 018-S 020-S; (2) restructure the feature into whatever shipment shape that decision requires; (3) backlogit move 018-S --status queued and move the member tasks to queued; (4) run impl-plan, plan-harden (REQUIRED - this feature is intrinsically complex) and plan-review on the resulting plan. Removing the edge without step 4 is a P-006 violation.

These are jointly unsatisfiable for any SHIPMENT whose covering feature has more than one QUEUED test-bearing task, because Ship Step 2 lists ALL queued tasks for the feature and harnesses them in one batch. See 021.001-T for the decision gate and its three admissible options. The retired rev-11 to rev-15 manifest / terminal-witness / -gatetask selector / MP0-MP6 machinery is NON-GOVERNING PROVENANCE ONLY and MUST NOT be resurrected without explicit re-deliberation.
