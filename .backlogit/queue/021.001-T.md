Parent feature 021-F. READINESS DECISION for all deferred Stage-enforcement work. Resolved by STAGE through deliberation plus plan review. NOT executed by Ship: it produces a decision artifact, not code. Until it is resolved, deferred features 019-F and 020-F and all their tasks remain status BLOCKED and belong to NO shipment - there is nothing for the Orchestrator to select or for Ship to claim.

THE PROBLEM TO DECIDE. P-004 requires EVERY generated test function to be red before implementation begins. _ship.agent.md Step 4.3 requires the FULL "go test ./..." to be green after EVERY task. Ship Step 2 item 1 lists ALL tasks for the target FEATURE that are in queued status and harnesses them in ONE batch. Therefore, for any covering feature with more than one QUEUED test-bearing task: H0 makes all N red, task 1 goes green, functions 2..N remain red by design, Step 4.3 fails, build-feature exhausts its attempts implementing future work, and no task can complete. Revisions 10 through 15 of the predecessor plan tried to bridge this with surface predicates, a harness-state manifest, a terminal witness, a -gatetask selector and an MP0-MP6 mutation-proof series, and failed adversarial review every time.

WHY THE EARLIER GUIDANCE WAS WRONG AND IS WITHDRAWN. The deferred plan previously recommended "split into a sequence of single-task SHIPMENT MANIFESTS". That is INVALID: Ship Step 2's harness batch is scoped to the covering FEATURE's queued tasks, not to the shipment manifest, so several single-task manifests over one multi-task feature still produce one N-function red batch and still deadlock at Step 4.3. That recommendation is WITHDRAWN.

ADMISSIBLE OPTIONS - CHOOSE ONE, WITH EVIDENCE. Do NOT pick a speculative mechanism now.
OPTION 1 - SINGLE-TASK FEATURES AND SHIPMENTS. Decompose each strand into covering features that each hold EXACTLY ONE queued task, one per shipment. Ship Step 2's feature-scoped listing then yields exactly one function per batch. Already proven in practice by the reduced 017-S. Requires no contract amendment. Cost: more features and shipments, more closure cycles, and each feature must be a genuinely coherent release unit rather than a wrapper around one task.
OPTION 2 - MACHINE-ENFORCED ONE-QUEUED-CHILD SERIALIZATION. Keep one covering feature per strand but guarantee that at most ONE child task is in queued status at any time, the rest blocked, promoted one at a time. MUST BE PROVEN, not asserted, against: (a) Ship Step 2 item 1's queued-status listing; (b) Ship Step 3 item 1's manifest executable-set derivation, whose exhaustive positive status rule KEEPS queued and active, reports archived as pre_archived_skipped and done as already_done, and FAILS CLOSED on ANY OTHER status - a blocked manifest member is therefore a HALT, not a skip, so blocked siblings must be kept OUT of the manifest or the rule must be amended; and (c) P-015 closure, where any covering-feature descendant left out of the manifest becomes a PROTECTED-SET member whose archived-or-missing state at the baseline integrity gate halts closure.
OPTION 3 - REVIEWED CONTRACT AMENDMENT MAKING HARNESS SELECTION SHIPMENT-SCOPED. Amend P-002/P-004, _ship.agent.md Step 2 and Step 4.3, and the harness-architect skill so the harness batch and the green-after-every-task gate are scoped to the SHIPMENT MANIFEST rather than to the covering feature. This is a deliberate safety-contract change to the rule that DETECTS the problem, and requires its own deliberation, its own risk assessment and its own review. It was expressly out of scope for 018-F.

FORBIDDEN WITHOUT EXPLICIT RE-DELIBERATION. Do not resurrect the retired rev-11 to rev-15 harness-state manifest, terminal witness, -gatetask selector, surface-predicate activation or MP0-MP6 series. Their defects are recorded as NON-GOVERNING provenance in the historical appendix of docs/plans/2026-09-11-intercom-go-stage-artifact-branch-pr-policy-gap-plan.md and must be read before any such proposal.
