READINESS DECISION STRAND - NOT A DELIVERY FEATURE.

PURPOSE. This feature exists solely to hold the single readiness-decision item (021.001-T) that gates every deferred Stage-enforcement feature. Its output is a REVIEWED DECISION ARTIFACT, not code.

NO SHIPMENT, NO LOCK TOKEN (corrected rev 18). The rev-17 construction modelled this gate as a LOCK-TOKEN SHIPMENT 020-S carrying blocks edges from 018-S and 019-S. That construction was INVALID and is WITHDRAWN: backlogit's shipment status enum is exactly queued|active|shipped|abandoned, so the "blocked" status those three records carried was never a valid shipment status - the generic "backlogit move" CLI accepted it only through a known defect (verified by execution: move <shipment> --status blocked exits 0; move <shipment> --status shipped exits 9). A lock token that can only exist in an invalid state is not enforcement. The dependency edges 018-S -> 020-S and 019-S -> 020-S were REMOVED, and 018-S, 019-S and 020-S were ARCHIVED with the official non-destructive "backlogit archive" operation. Nothing was deleted; the archived records preserve their manifests and full traceability.

WHAT REPLACES THE LOCK. Deferred features 019-F and 020-F, and all their tasks, are status BLOCKED and belong to NO shipment. Orchestrator Step 2 and Ship Step 0.5 both operate on SHIPMENTS: with no shipment there is nothing to list, select, claim or mis-route. This is strictly safer than any queued-shipment representation, because a queued shipment is by definition claimable and there is no valid queued-shipment readiness lock in current backlogit semantics unless a genuine predecessor shipment is actually intended to ship. It is acceptable, and safer, to retain blocked future features and tasks with no live shipment until implementation readiness.

UNLOCK PROCEDURE (STAGE ONLY). After 021.001-T records a REVIEWED decision: Stage restructures the target feature into the shape that decision requires, runs impl-plan, plan-harden (REQUIRED) and plan-review, and ONLY THEN creates a fresh shipment over the reviewed shape and moves its member tasks to queued. Creating a shipment before plan review is a P-006 violation. Ship and the Orchestrator never perform any of this.
