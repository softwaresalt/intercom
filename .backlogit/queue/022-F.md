WHY THE THIRD FILE. Without it the agent role table and the policy registry disagree about the same authority, so Ship would be simultaneously authorized and unauthorized to perform the one transition this feature exists to enable. The expansion is limited to that single bullet; every other clause of P-010 and every other policy is explicitly out of scope, and negative test rows assert no further widening and no release-instance identifier.

WHY THE FOURTH FILE. The installed skill exposes no pre-mutation verdict boundary: its close-path classification lives inside mode safe-close Step 0(c), and on a non-CASCADE selection it continues directly into the mutating safe-close steps, while the verdict is recorded only after the destructive cascade call. A caller therefore cannot refuse a close path before a mutation has already happened. OPTION A adds a read-only public mode, classify-close-path, that runs the same Step 0 logic, STOPS at the dispatch, and returns CLOSE_PATH_VERDICT (CASCADE, SAFE_CLOSE or BLOCK) with reason, evidence and a CLASSIFICATION_BINDING that mode safe-close revalidates before any mutation. The four sites are ADDITIVE: all four pre-existing public modes and the legacy unbound fall-through are preserved verbatim and asserted by preservation test rows.
