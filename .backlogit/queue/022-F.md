WHY THE FOURTH FILE. The installed skill exposes no pre-mutation verdict boundary: its close-path classification lives inside mode safe-close Step 0(c), and on a non-CASCADE selection it continues directly into the mutating safe-close steps, while the verdict is recorded only after the destructive cascade call. A caller therefore cannot refuse a close path before a mutation has already happened. OPTION A adds a read-only public mode, classify-close-path, that runs the same Step 0 logic, STOPS at the dispatch, and returns CLOSE_PATH_VERDICT (CASCADE, SAFE_CLOSE or BLOCK) with reason, evidence and a CLASSIFICATION_BINDING that mode safe-close revalidates before any mutation. Three of the four sites are ADDITIVE: all four pre-existing public modes keep their names, inputs and internal steps. The single non-additive change is D-1, ADOPTED BY THE OPERATOR 2026-09-14: mode safe-close invoked WITHOUT a valid binding now REFUSES and mutates nothing, so the legacy unbound fall-through is deliberately REMOVED rather than preserved.
