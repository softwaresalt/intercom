SCOPE - .github/agents/_orchestrator.agent.md Step 1.5 (Staging Artifact Merge Gate). Correct the gate so it can actually consume a Stage-produced artifact branch.
THE DEFECT BEING FIXED. The installed Step 1.5 is MAIN-SCOPED and cannot see a Stage side branch. Its step 1 inspects the working tree; its step 2 evaluates "git log origin/main..main", which is EMPTY whenever Stage worked on chore/stage-{scope-slug}, so the gate concludes "local and remote are in sync" and skips its own commit/push/PR arm entirely - then fails at step 4 ("git show origin/main:.backlogit/queue/{shipment_id}.md") with STAGING_GATE_FAIL. That fail-closed halt is correct and must be PRESERVED as the terminal for the unhandled case, but it makes the gate unable to complete a normal Stage handoff without manual intervention.
(1) BRANCH DISCOVERY: consume the Stage handback record emitted by 019.007-T (stage_branch, stage_base_commit, stage_head_commit, stage_outcome, stage_artifact_paths, shipment_id) instead of inferring state from main. Discover the Stage branch by NAME from that record, never by scanning or guessing.
(2) AUTHORIZATION: state explicitly which actor may commit, push and open the staging PR, and under whose authority. The Orchestrator's existing Step 1.5 item 3 already directs it to do so; this task makes that authority explicit and consistent with P-010, and records the OPERATOR as the always-available substitute actor for direct Stage invocation. It MUST NOT silently widen the Orchestrator role boundary: if the chosen wording grants the Orchestrator anything it does not already hold, that grant is a separate, deliberated P-010 amendment and is OUT OF SCOPE here.
(3) INTERNAL CONTRADICTION: step 3 currently says "commit to a staging branch and open a PR" in items (a)-(d) while item (e) says "attempt a direct push to main first". Resolve it in favour of the branch-and-PR path; a direct default-branch push by the Orchestrator is exactly the class of write this whole feature set exists to prevent.
(4) SLUG CONSISTENCY: step 3(a) names the branch chore/stage-{shipment_id}, but Step 1.9 creates chore/stage-{scope-slug} and the shipment may not exist yet when the branch is created. Use the discovered stage_branch name from the handback record.
(5) CONCRETE POST-MERGE VERIFICATION: after the staging PR merges, verify the shipment manifest on the remote default branch and verify the handback's stage_artifact_paths are all present there, with a named failure token on mismatch.
(6) PRESERVE THE FAIL-CLOSED TERMINAL: when no handback record is available, the gate still halts rather than proceeding or inferring.

DEPENDS ON 019.007-T (the handback record it consumes) and 018.008-T (the branch-naming contract).
