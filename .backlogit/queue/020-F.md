STRAND B - MECHANICAL ENFORCEMENT OF THE STAGE DEFAULT-BRANCH CONTRACT. DEFERRED, BLOCKED, NO SHIPMENT.

WHY THIS FEATURE EXISTS. The contract corrected by 018-F lives in autoharness-GENERATED files (.github/policies/workflow-policies.md and .github/agents/_stage.agent.md). A regeneration can silently revert it. 018-F accepts that as an explicit interim residual risk. This feature removes the risk by making the corrected contract mechanically enforced and regeneration-resistant.

SCOPE (five tasks): 020.001-T direct-push fixture corpus and manifest; 020.002-T fixture-suite driver over a stubbed detector; 020.003-T structure-aware direct-push detector; 020.004-T CI lint-job wiring, divergence ledger and CODEOWNERS; 020.005-T coupling verification with an executed mutation proof.

WHY IT IS SEPARATE FROM 019-F. Until 2026-09-12 both strands lived in one feature that mixed PERSISTENCE SEMANTICS (what Stage and the Orchestrator do at handback) with REUSABLE ENFORCEMENT INFRASTRUCTURE (detector, fixtures, event parsing, CI, regeneration proof). They have different consumers, different blast radius and different review needs, and bundling them is what let the original release unit grow to eight tasks. After the split neither strand depends on the other: this feature depends only on 018.008-T and its own internal chain.

SCOPE DISCIPLINE - THE ORIGINAL FAILURE MODE. Detector over-generalization was the scope driver that inflated the retired eight-task unit. Constrain the detector to the CORRECTED CONTRACT CORPUS. Prefer targeted assertions over a generalized platform. A fixture "platform" is NOT a goal; a fixture corpus sufficient to prove the corrected contract is.

NO LIVE SHIPMENT - THIS IS THE ENFORCEMENT, AND IT IS DELIBERATE (rev 18). The rev-17 readiness lock used shipment 019-S with status "blocked" plus a blocks edge to lock-token shipment 020-S. That construction was INVALID: backlogit's shipment status enum is exactly queued|active|shipped|abandoned, so "blocked" is not a valid shipment status - the generic "backlogit move" CLI accepted it only through a known defect (verified by execution: move <shipment> --status blocked exits 0; move <shipment> --status shipped exits 9). There is NO safe valid queued-shipment readiness lock in current backlogit semantics unless a genuine predecessor shipment is actually intended to ship, and a queued shipment is by definition claimable. 019-S and lock token 020-S were therefore ARCHIVED with the official non-destructive "backlogit archive" operation, their blocks edges removed first, and nothing was deleted. A blocked FEATURE with blocked TASKS and NO shipment is the safe representation: Orchestrator Step 2 and Ship Step 0.5 both operate on shipments, and there is no shipment to select, claim, or mis-route. Verified: "backlogit queue view" lists 017-S as the only shipment in the workspace.

HOW STAGE RECONSTITUTES THIS FEATURE. Only after 021.001-T records a REVIEWED decision selecting a valid harness execution model, Stage (never Ship, never the Orchestrator) performs, in order: (1) restructure the feature into whatever shape that decision requires; (2) run impl-plan, plan-harden (REQUIRED) and plan-review; (3) only then CREATE A FRESH SHIPMENT over the reviewed shape and move the member tasks to queued. Creating a shipment before step 2 is a P-006 violation. Members of an archived shipment remain freely re-assemblable into a new shipment (verified by execution).
