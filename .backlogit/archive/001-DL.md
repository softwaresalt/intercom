# D-2: 022.001-T IV-5 harness methodology - fixture-backed behavioral tests replace substring needles

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
Frozen IV-5 requires the TDD harness to PROVE that refusal paths perform zero mutation and that valid CASCADE/SAFE_CLOSE paths preserve intended behaviour. The methodology currently calibrated in plan revision 13 for 022.001-T is a 33-row table-driven Go test asserting pinned SUBSTRING needles over three markdown instruction-surface files (.github/agents/_ship.agent.md, .github/policies/workflow-policies.md, .github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md). A static text assertion can only prove that prose is PRESENT or ABSENT. It cannot observe a mutation, cannot observe the absence of a mutation, and cannot exercise classify-close-path, safe-close or the cascade sub-procedure. Therefore the substring harness is structurally incapable of satisfying IV-5, and a green substring suite would be a false GREEN against a BLOCKING invariant. Source refs: stash E47FFCE7, PR #54 (merged f7112e0), thread PRRT_kwDOTPuhps6irrfr, shipment 021-S, feature 022-F, task 022.001-T, invariant IV-5 (plan section 12.1), residual N-6 (soft-wrap needle fragility) and N-5 (assertion must resolve inside its own subject).
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
OPTION 1 (REJECTED) - Keep the 33-row substring harness as the IV-5 acceptance surface. Cheapest, matches frozen plan section 4.2/6.1 prose and the S1a/S1b timing calibration. REJECTED: structurally cannot observe mutation or non-mutation, so it cannot discharge IV-5; also inherits residual N-6 (pinned needles wrap mid-span in the soft-wrapped section 4.2 source) and N-5 (cross-credited assertions).
OPTION 2 (REJECTED) - Amend 022.001-T prose to declare the substring harness sufficient for IV-5. REJECTED: IV-1..IV-5 are FROZEN and GOVERN over any conflicting AC-, clause site or pinned wording; they are explicitly not dischargeable by editing plan or record prose (plan section 12.1 Blocking rule). This option is a relabel, not a fix.
OPTION 3 (ADOPTED) - Fixture-backed BEHAVIORAL harness. Ship writes Go tests that materialise a disposable fixture backlog workspace, invoke the real classify-close-path / safe-close / cascade flows against it, capture a pre-state snapshot and a post-state snapshot of the backlog tree, and assert the state delta. Refusal rows assert delta == EMPTY. Valid CASCADE/SAFE_CLOSE rows assert the intended delta exactly. The substring rows are DEMOTED to supplemental prompt-contract checks that prove clause-site coverage only.
OPTION 4 (CONSIDERED, NOT ADOPTED AS PRIMARY) - Split the harness into a separate backlog task. NOT ADOPTED: any second task under 022-F becomes a depth-1 live descendant, forces a third member into the 021-S manifest, live-mismatches PA-021-CASCADE condition (3) and voids the operator approval as a re-scoped action. The existing S1a/S1b/S2 execution split absorbs the work instead.
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
ADOPT OPTION 3 - BEHAVIORAL TESTS ARE THE IV-5 ACCEPTANCE SURFACE. Ship's RED harness for 022.001-T MUST be fixture-backed behavioral tests that actually invoke the classify-close-path, safe-close and cascade flows and compare captured pre/post backlog state. Static substring/text-needle checks over the three instruction-surface files MAY remain, but ONLY as SUPPLEMENTAL prompt-contract coverage checks; they CANNOT satisfy IV-5 and MUST NOT be counted toward it. NO PLAN REVISION 14 IS REQUIRED: plan section 12.1 already makes IV-1..IV-5 govern over any conflicting AC-, clause-site, pinned-wording or measurement statement, and the operator-accepted residual disposition of 2026-09-14 already records that the harness, not the prose, is authoritative at build time. Row counts (33), clause-site counts (16+2) and the S1a/S1b/S2 timing calibration are EVIDENCE RESIDUALS, not the acceptance surface, and are NOT relabelled or recounted here.
