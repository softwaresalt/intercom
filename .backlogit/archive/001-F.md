First shippable, fork-independent slice of the agent-intercom Rust-to-Go port. Establishes the Go module, two binary entrypoints, canonical CGO-free 4-target cross-compilation, and a hardened CI pipeline (build, vet, race, gofmt, goimports, golangci-lint, staticcheck, govulncheck, secret scanning, cross-compile matrix). Every unit is required verbatim under either candidate architecture, so this slice carries no exposure to the unresolved architecture fork. Config schema, credential resolution, and the AppError taxonomy were deliberately EXCLUDED by plan review (deferred to stash 037B1552) because they are Group-A-specific and should be validated against the Rust behavioral oracle before being frozen as operator-facing contracts.
