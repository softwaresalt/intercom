AC5 execution confined to t.TempDir(); recorded that the probe deliberately runs outside the workspace tree. AC6 CI asserts INTERCOM_LIVE_SDK_TESTS unset in every workflow job and absent from all committed config. AC7 with the var unset 'go test ./...' passes with zero network or credential access.
