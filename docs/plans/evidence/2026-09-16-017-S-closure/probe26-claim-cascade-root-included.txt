### FINDINGS CARRIED INTO THE CLOSURE PLAN
1. The claim cascade transitions the ROOT COVERING FEATURE queued -> active on a
   root-included manifest whose siblings are genuinely status: archived. This is the
   direct evidence for section 3.2 condition 5 and step S-3, replacing the prior
   reliance on AC-19 (a normative requirement, not a measurement) and on 021-S's
   2-member manifest.
2. The claim cascade SKIPS members declaring status: archived. All 11 retained
   loc=archive, status=archived, archived_status=queued, parent_id intact. The
   section 3.3 pre-archived exemption and section 3.2 condition 2 are therefore NOT
   disturbed by the claim.
3. The claim cascade also activates the live TASK member (001.004-T). Ship's Step 4.1
   task claim is pre-empted by the engine for manifest tasks; the closure plan must
   disclose this rather than prescribe an ordering the engine has already performed.
4. Nothing outside the manifest was touched by the claim.
5. TOOLING: `backlogit move <id> --status archived` is NOT an archive operation.
   Use `backlogit archive <id>`. Any probe or runbook step that archives via `move`
   silently produces a non-archived record.
